You are my senior full-stack engineer + compliance architect working inside my Client Vault app.

NEW FEATURE:
Add an AI-assisted “Contradictions Engine” that detects and documents contradictions between:
- representations / commitments / promises (what was said/written)
vs
- later statements, actions, non-performance, or documentary evidence (what actually occurred)

This engine must be EVIDENCE-BOUND, COURT-READY, and SAFE:
- It MUST NOT invent facts.
- It MUST NOT speculate about intent.
- It MUST NOT accuse or label crimes.
- It MUST quote only from stored artifacts (documents, messages, transcripts, call transcripts, timeline notes) and must attach exhibit references.
- It MUST produce a neutral “Observed inconsistency” memo and an optional “Clarification Request” letter that is non-threatening and purely administrative.

PRIMARY OUTCOME:
For each Enforcement Case, produce a “Contradictions Dossier” that:
- lists contradictions as pairs of quotes with exhibit IDs
- includes a short neutral explanation of why the statements cannot both be true (fact-only reasoning)
- identifies missing evidence needed to resolve the inconsistency
- supports (a) civil enforcement record and (b) referral packet readiness (internal only)

PART C — AI CONTRADICTION ANALYST (INTERNAL ONLY)

Implement server/contradictions/aiAnalyst.ts

INPUTS (STRICT):
- enforcement_case record snapshot (facts)
- evidence snippets with source IDs, timestamps, and allowed excerpt length
- DO NOT provide the AI raw unlimited content; provide curated candidate excerpts and metadata.

AI TASK:
- Propose contradiction candidates by pairing two excerpts that appear inconsistent.
- For each candidate:
  - supply side A quote + exhibit reference
  - supply side B quote + exhibit reference
  - neutral explanation (why both can’t be true)
  - list open questions + evidence needed to resolve
  - confidence + severity

GUARDRAILS:
- No speculation.
- No accusations.
- No legal conclusions.
- No invented quotes.
- Output must include ONLY the provided excerpts and references.
- If uncertain, mark “needs_more_evidence”.

STORE OUTPUT:
- Save candidates as contradiction_items (status=candidate) with evidence links.
- Admin must confirm items to mark “confirmed”.

PART D — SCORING + THRESHOLDS

Compute score_total for contradiction_set:
- minor=2, material=5, critical=10

Thresholds:
- watch >= 15
- elevated >= 30
- critical >= 60
Use these only for INTERNAL banners and referral readiness suggestions.
