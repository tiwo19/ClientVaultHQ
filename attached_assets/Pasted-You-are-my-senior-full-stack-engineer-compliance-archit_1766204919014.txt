You are my senior full-stack engineer + compliance architect working inside my existing “Client Vault” Replit app.

GOAL
Build an “Administrative Enforcement & Estoppel Engine” for non-performing notes / contracts that produces a notarized, court-ready administrative record (timeline + evidence + exports). This is NOT a template system: letters must be AI-generated, neutral, jurisdiction-safe, and strictly grounded in the case record. The system must also generate a sworn “Silence / Non-Response” affidavit, and map workflows for small claims → circuit court, plus collections / lien / judgment integrations.

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES
1) Fact-bound outputs only: AI may only state facts that exist in the Client Vault record for that enforcement case. No invention, no speculation.
2) Neutral, jurisdiction-safe tone: no threats, no accusations, no inflammatory language.
3) Procedural fairness: every letter provides clear notice + opportunity to cure + clear response deadline.
4) Evidence integrity: notarized and sent artifacts are immutable (no deletes/overwrites; versioned corrections only).
5) Court readiness: one-click evidence binder export (PDF + ZIP + CSV) with hashes, chronology, delivery proofs.
6) Human in the loop: declaring Default and Estoppel requires explicit admin confirmation clicks.
