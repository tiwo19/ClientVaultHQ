Replit Agent Prompt
“Administrative Enforcement & Estoppel Engine” (Client Vault)

You are my senior legal-systems engineer.
You will implement a formal Administrative Enforcement & Estoppel Engine inside Client Vault to manage non-performing notes, contracts, and agreements.

This system must create a notarized, auditable, chronological administrative record that:

Places parties on lawful notice

Provides clear opportunity to cure

Documents non-performance

Establishes estoppel through silence, admission, or failure to rebut

Produces a court-ready evidence packet requiring minimal judicial interpretation

This is not legal advice generation — it is administrative process orchestration and record preservation.

I. Core Legal Strategy Model (System Doctrine)

The system must enforce the following administrative doctrine:

“Notice + Opportunity + Silence or Failure = Estoppel”

Every enforcement action must be structured so that:

The counterparty cannot later claim lack of notice

The counterparty cannot claim ambiguity

The counterparty cannot claim lack of opportunity to cure

The record shows procedural fairness

The counterparty’s failure becomes self-establishing evidence

III. Administrative Notice Ladder (CRITICAL)

Implement a mandatory notice sequence (configurable but opinionated):

Notice Tier 1 — Administrative Notice of Record

Purpose: Establish record, identify obligation, request clarification or performance.

Identifies:

Agreement

Obligations

Current deficiency

Requests:

Written response

Evidence of performance

Deadline: 10–15 days

Sent via:

Certified mail

Email (logged)

Optional courier

System requirements:

Letter generated from structured data

PDF rendered

Notary block required

Notary details logged

Mailing receipt uploaded & hashed

Notice Tier 2 — Notice of Opportunity to Cure

Purpose: Demonstrate good faith and fairness.

Explicitly states:

Prior notice sent

No satisfactory response received

Provides:

Specific cure actions

Exact cure deadline

Warns:

Failure will result in default declaration

System requirements:

Cannot be sent unless Tier 1 exists

Deadline enforced by system clock

Evidence lock on Tier 1 artifacts

Notice Tier 3 — Notice of Default & Demand

Purpose: Declare default formally.

States:

Default has occurred

Cure period expired

Demands:

Immediate performance OR

Written rebuttal with evidence

Introduces:

Estoppel language (failure to rebut = admission)

System requirements:

Requires explicit admin confirmation

Triggers default_declared status

All prior artifacts locked

Notice Tier 4 — Notice of Estoppel & Administrative Determination

Purpose: Close the record.

States:

Notices sent

Deadlines passed

No rebuttal received

Declares:

Facts deemed admitted

Defenses waived by silence

Provides:

Final opportunity to contest administratively

System requirements:

Marks case as estoppel_established

Evidence bundle auto-prepared
